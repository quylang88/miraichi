# Responsible Use Policy

Rules to prevent addiction and promote safe betting practices.

## Purpose
Ensures that the application encourages safety, budgeting, and self-assessment.

## Status
- **Status**: Draft

## Scope
User-facing safety disclosures, warning boards, and self-exclusion options.

## Policy Rules
- Display a responsible use disclaimer prominently on the dashboard.
- Allow users to set daily, weekly, or monthly loss limits.
- Offer self-exclusion options to disable slips tracking temporarily.
